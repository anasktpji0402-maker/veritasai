      Veritas AI

      Veritas AI is a privacy-first scam and misinformation awareness prototype. It evaluates text and URLs using local heuristic signals and explains the reasons behind a risk assessment.

      This prototype does not connect to a live threat-intelligence database, search engine, news fact-checking service, or external AI model. A result is an indicator—not proof that content is true or false.
